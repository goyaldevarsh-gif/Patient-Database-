How to request deletion

OrthoVault is a personal patient-record app used by an individual orthopaedic surgeon. Accounts are created and managed by the practising doctor, not self-registered by patients. To request deletion of a doctor account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

  1. Send an email to orthovault1@gmail.com from the email address registered with your OrthoVault account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include your registered account email and, if possible, the approximate date the account was created.
  4. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion request within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

Data typeAction
Account credentials (email, login)Permanently deleted
Patient case records created by this accountPermanently deleted
Uploaded photos / imagesPermanently deleted
Cloud backups tied to the accountPermanently deleted

Deletion is irreversible. Locally exported backups on your own device are not affected and must be removed separately by you.

Retention period

Once a deletion request is confirmed, associated data is removed from our active systems within 7 business days. Residual copies in routine encrypted backups (if any) are purged within 30 days as part of standard backup rotation.

Need help or have questions about this process? Contact orthovault1@gmail.com and we'll assist you directly.
